# Exploratory review

## Proportionality

Assess proportionality for this local, single-user, solo-developer project. Prefer simple, low-maintenance solutions that satisfy the user's actual workflow. Do not recommend enterprise patterns, exhaustive handling, or extra process unless their concrete utility justifies the maintenance and technical-debt cost.

Use this optional review to assess whether the changed approach is understandable and proportionate.

Cover the problem and approach, notable strengths, fragile areas, non-blocking maintainability concerns, useful author questions, and optional improvements. Do not turn preferences into compliance failures.
